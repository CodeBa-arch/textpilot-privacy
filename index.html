Política de Privacidade - TextPilot

Última atualização: Outubro de 2026

TextPilot ("we", "our", or "extension") respects and values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our browser extension for Google Chrome.

By installing and using TextPilot, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect and Store

TextPilot was designed with a strong focus on privacy and data minimization:

  • Snippets and Local Settings: Your text shortcuts (snippets), replacement texts, and configuration preferences are saved primarily and securely in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage) to ensure fast and offline functionality.
  • Account and Authentication (Optional): If you use login or account synchronization features (integrated via secure authentication services such as Firebase), we may process basic account identifiers (such as email address and user ID) strictly to manage your session and sync preferences across devices.
  • Web Page Interaction: The extension injects content scripts strictly necessary to monitor text fields in real-time, detecting when a shortcut trigger is typed to perform automatic text replacement. Content typed outside of triggers is not collected, monitored, or transmitted to external servers.

2. How We Use Information

Information handled by the extension is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the core text expansion and shortcut management features.
  • To enable user authentication and secure account data synchronization (when applicable).
  • To improve stability, performance, and user experience.

3. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell, trade, rent, or transfer your personal information or usage data to third parties. Your data belongs exclusively to you. Data sharing is strictly limited to essential and secure technology infrastructure services (such as cloud authentication/database providers like Firebase) necessary to power online features, complying with rigorous security standards.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and administrative measures to protect data processed by TextPilot against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All communication with support or authentication servers occurs over encrypted channels (HTTPS).

5. Extension Permissions

TextPilot requests only the permissions strictly necessary to deliver its productivity features:

  • Storage: To save your shortcuts and preferences locally.
  • Scripting / Host Permissions: To detect shortcuts and perform text replacement in editable fields across webpages you visit.
  • Clipboard / ContextMenus / SidePanel (as applicable): To facilitate user-initiated actions (such as copying texts quickly or managing snippets in the side panel).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements or regulatory changes. We encourage you to review this page from time to time. The "Last updated" date will always reflect the most recent changes.
